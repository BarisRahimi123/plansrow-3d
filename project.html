/ 01 — THE CHALLENGE

No reliable record of 200,000 sq ft.

The redevelopment team lacked accurate structural and architectural documentation for over 200,000 sq ft of the existing mall. With plans to renovate the structure and build 600 new apartment units directly above it, designing and permitting against unverified conditions carried real risk — every assumption was a potential change order.

/ 02 — OUR APPROACH

Capture once. Model everything.

Plansrow delivered a complete 3D laser scan with LOD 400 as-built documentation — capturing the building to a level of detail engineers could build from. The point cloud was modeled into coordinated Revit and ArchiCAD models, supported by aerial imagery, a virtual walkthrough, and detailed structural mapping right down to beam sizes, bolt counts, and welds.

/ 03 — THE RESULT

Design and permit with confidence.

Developers and engineers gained full visibility into the existing structure — the confidence to move through design, permitting, and construction with risk and guesswork removed. One scan became the single source of truth for a complex vertical addition.
